# Mobile app

The FlowDesk mobile app needs iOS 15 or later, or Android 10 or later. Update it from the App Store or Google Play.

If the app crashes on launch: update the app, restart the phone, then reinstall it. Your data syncs from the cloud, so nothing is lost. On Android you can also clear the app cache.

Push notifications: allow them in the phone settings and turn them on in the app under Settings > Notifications.

The mobile app does not have an offline mode. An internet connection is required.
