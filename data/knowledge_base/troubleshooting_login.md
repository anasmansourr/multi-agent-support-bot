# Troubleshooting login problems

If you cannot log in: check the email address, check Caps Lock, then use "Forgot password".

If you are logged out again and again, your browser may be blocking cookies. Allow cookies for flowdesk.example and disable privacy extensions for the site. Sessions normally last 14 days.

If Sign in with Google loops back to the login page, allow third-party cookies for accounts.google.com and try again.

If the 2FA code does not arrive: SMS can take up to 2 minutes. For authenticator apps, set the phone clock to automatic time, because codes depend on it. You can also use a backup code.

If the account is locked, wait 15 minutes. If it is still locked, contact support.
