# FlowDesk overview

FlowDesk is a project-management app for teams. (FlowDesk is a fictional product created for this course project; these documents are demo content.)

Core features: tasks and subtasks, kanban board view, list view, dashboards, comments, file attachments, notifications, CSV import and export, a public REST API and webhooks.

FlowDesk runs in the browser (latest two versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge) and as mobile apps for iOS 15 or later and Android 10 or later.

Integrations: Slack, Google Calendar and GitHub. Plans: Starter, Pro, Business and Enterprise (see the plans and pricing document).
