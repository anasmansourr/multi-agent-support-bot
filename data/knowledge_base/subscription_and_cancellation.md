# Subscriptions and cancellation

Cancel at any time under Settings > Billing > Cancel subscription. There is no cancellation fee. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for, and automatic renewal stops.

After a subscription ends, your data is kept for 30 days and can be restored by resubscribing. After that it is deleted.

Upgrades take effect immediately and you pay a prorated amount for the rest of the billing period.

Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing period. There is no credit for the current period.

Adding seats is prorated. Removing seats takes effect at the next renewal.

There is no pause feature. To stop billing, cancel the subscription and resubscribe later.
