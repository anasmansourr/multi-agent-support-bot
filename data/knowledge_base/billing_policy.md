# Billing policy

Monthly plans are billed in advance on the same day of the month as sign-up. Annual plans are billed in advance once a year.

Payment methods: Visa, Mastercard, American Express and PayPal. Business and Enterprise customers can pay by invoice with bank transfer (30 days).

All charges are in US dollars only. Your bank may show a different amount if it converts the currency.

Invoices are emailed on the charge date and can be downloaded under Settings > Billing > Invoices. Change the billing email under Settings > Billing.

Taxes: VAT, GST or sales tax is added based on your billing address. Add a tax ID under Settings > Billing to be treated as tax exempt where the law allows.

Failed payments are retried after 3 and after 5 days, and you get an email each time. A $10 late fee is added when an invoice is 7 days overdue. At 30 days overdue the account becomes read-only. Paying the invoice restores full access immediately.

Promo codes: apply one code per account at checkout or under Settings > Billing. A code applies to the first payment only unless the offer says otherwise.
