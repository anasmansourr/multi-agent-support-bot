# Refund policy

You can request a refund within 30 days of a payment. This applies to monthly and annual plans.

Refunds go back to the original payment method and take 5 to 10 business days to appear.

Payments older than 30 days are not refundable. Seats are not prorated for part of a month.

A duplicate charge is refunded in full, whatever its age.

Only a human billing specialist can issue a refund. Email billing@flowdesk.example with your invoice number and the reason for the request.
