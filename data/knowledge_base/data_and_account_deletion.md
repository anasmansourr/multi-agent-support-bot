# Data export and account deletion

Export your data under Settings > Data > Export, as CSV or JSON. The export is ready within 1 hour and the download link works for 7 days.

Delete an account under Settings > Account > Delete account. Only the account owner can do this. There is a 30-day grace period in which you can restore the account. After that, data is permanently deleted, and it disappears from backups within 90 days.
