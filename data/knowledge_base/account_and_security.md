# Account and security

Reset a password: choose "Forgot password" on the login page. The reset link in the email works for 30 minutes.

Two-factor authentication (2FA) works with an authenticator app or SMS. Save your backup codes when you turn it on. If you lose your phone, sign in with a backup code or contact support to verify your identity.

Sign in with Google is available. SAML single sign-on (SSO) is not available yet and is planned for Q4 2026.

After 5 failed login attempts the account is locked for 15 minutes.

Roles: Admin, Member and Guest. Guests are invited by email and need an account.

Security: data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2 or later) and at rest (AES-256). FlowDesk is GDPR compliant. You choose a data region, US or EU, when you create the account.
