# Plans and pricing

Prices are per seat per month in US dollars.

- Starter: $29 per seat. Up to 5 projects and 5 GB of storage.
- Pro: $49 per seat. Unlimited projects and 50 GB of storage.
- Business: $79 per seat. Unlimited projects, 500 GB of storage, advanced permissions and priority support.
- Enterprise: custom price. Adds a service level agreement, a dedicated manager and 24/7 support. Contact sales through support.

There is no permanent free plan. Every new account gets a 14-day free trial of Pro, and no credit card is needed to start it.

Annual billing gives two months free (you pay for ten months).

Non-profit organisations and educational institutions get 20% off Pro and Business. Ask support and send proof of status.

Extra storage costs $5 per month for each additional 50 GB.
